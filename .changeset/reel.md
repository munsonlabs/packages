Experimental first cut of reel: `createClip()` trims a video, crops it to 9:16 (or any aspect) and exports an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio entirely in the browser with WebCodecs and Mediabunny. AAC audio is copied without re-encoding and other audio is encoded as AAC; where this browser has no AAC encoder (Firefox), a source with other audio gives a silent clip and `onWarning({ reason: 'audio-unavailable', target: 'audio', message })` (`console.warn` by default). An abort that lands while the file is being finalised still rejects with the signal's reason. Sources are a `Blob`, a URL or a `<video>`. `canClip()` reads only a source's header and answers what would happen to the audio (`plan.audio`: `'copy'`, `'encode'`, `'none'` or `'unavailable'`) or, as a stable `reason` code, why it cannot be clipped (an embed page, DRM, MediaSource, a MediaStream, DASH, a cross-origin file without CORS, an unreadable container, an undecodable video, no H.264 encoder, no WebCodecs); `createClip` rejects with a `ClipError` carrying the same code. `support()` reports the H.264 encoder and decoder and the AAC encoder this browser has.

Captions burned into the picture: `captions: { cues, style }` takes parsed cues, WebVTT text, a `TextTrack`, or the URL of a WebVTT file (a `URL` or a string that is not caption text), fetched with the clip's signal; captions are WebVTT only, so convert SRT to WebVTT first. `captions.tracks` takes several tracks in video-player's `tracks` shape and burns in the one marked `default`, else the one matching `navigator.language`, else the first. Captions have one look: white bold text on a translucent dark box behind each line, 4.5% of the frame's height, 14% above the bottom, wrapping at 86% of the width onto as many lines as a cue needs, made for desktop and TV viewing. `style` is a `CaptionStyle` of overrides on it: `fontFamily`, `fontWeight`, `size` (a fraction of the height), `color`, `background` (or `null` for no box), `position` (`'top'`, `'middle'` or `'bottom'`), `margin` and `maxWidth`; a host posting to TikTok or Reels can raise the captions with `position` and `margin`. Captions that will not load never fail a clip: a failed fetch (an HTTP error, CORS) or a file that is not WebVTT (no `WEBVTT` header) leaves the clip without captions and calls `onWarning({ reason: 'captions-unavailable', target: 'captions', message })` (`console.warn` by default); `loadCaptions`, called directly, rejects with an `Error` saying why. New exports `parseVtt`, `isCaptionText`, `toCues` and `loadCaptions`.

Clips point back at their moment: `clipLink(origin, start, end)` is the origin URL with a Media Fragments `#t=start,end` (the link `@munsonlabs/video-player`'s deep links answer), and with an `origin` a clip's metadata carries the title, the publisher, that deep link and an encoder tag (MP4 atoms); `metadata: false` leaves them out. A source's own tags are never copied.

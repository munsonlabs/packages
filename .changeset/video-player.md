'@munsonlabs/video-player': minor

Clip ranges and deep links. `setClipRange(range, { end })` on the player handle (and on `<ml-video-player>`) highlights `{ start, end }` on the scrubber and, once the playhead is inside it, pauses once at its end (`'pause'`, the default), loops until the viewer seeks more than a second away (`'loop'`) or plays on (`'continue'`); `null` clears it. On native sources (MP4, HLS, DASH) the end is checked on every presented frame while playing (`requestVideoFrameCallback`, else `requestAnimationFrame`), so a loop or pause lands within about a frame of the end; embeds keep `timeupdate`. The range is on the handle as `clipRange`, and the handle's new read-only `mediaElement` is the native `<video>` (`null` for embeds), for reading time per frame. Opt-in deep links: with `deep-link` (or `deepLink`), a player answers a page URL pointing at a moment, `#ml-t=42,52` (the one format, in the hash, as `@munsonlabs/reel` clip links write it): it scrolls into view, seeks to the start and sets the link as its clip range, ending as `deep-link-end` says (`pause`, `loop` or `continue`). Give it an id (`deep-link="hero"`) and add `&ml-player=hero` to target one player among several; a link without an id goes to the first deep-link player. New export `parseDeepLink()`, new types `ClipRange`, `ClipRangeEnd`, `ClipRangeOptions`, `DeepLinkEnd` and `DeepLink`.

New export `useResolvedPlayer(props)` (and the type `ResolvedPlayerProps`) for controls built outside the package: it resolves `player`, then `for`, then the enclosing `VideoPlayer`.

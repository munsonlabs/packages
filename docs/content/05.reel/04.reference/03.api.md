## `@munsonlabs/reel`

### `createStoryboard`

Thumbnails come from the smallest video track at least as big as a tile (`track`, as in [`createClip`](/reel/making-clips/create-clip#hls-and-several-video-tracks)). By default each one is the **nearest keyframe at or before** its time: keyframes are looked up first, thumbnails sharing one are decoded once, and nothing after a keyframe is decoded. `exact: true` decodes to each exact time instead, which on a stream with a keyframe every few seconds costs many decodes per thumbnail. `onTile(index, image, time)` is called as each thumbnail is drawn, so a filmstrip can paint progressively; `image` is reused once the callback returns. Aborting `signal` disposes the input, cancelling downloads in flight, and rejects with the signal's reason. The output (sprite, VTT, `tileWidth`, `tileHeight`, `count`) is the same either way.

### Types

`CaptionStyle` (`{ fontFamily?, fontWeight?, size?, color?, background?, position?, margin?, maxWidth? }`, overrides on the caption look), `ClipOptions`, `TrackChoice`, `VideoTrackInfo`, `CaptionTrackInfo` (`{ id, kind: 'passed' | 'hls' | 'text-track', language, label, default }`), `CaptionInput` (`CaptionCue[] | string | URL | TextTrack`), `CaptionTrackSource` (`{ src: string | URL, kind?, srclang?, label?, default? }`, video-player's `CaptionTrackDef` shape), `PlaylistCache`, `StoryboardOptions`, `EndCardOptions` (with `displayUrl`), `EndCardInfo`, `StampOptions`, `StampPosition`, `ClipWarning` (`{ reason: 'logo-unavailable' | 'captions-unavailable', target: 'endCard' | 'stamp' | 'captions', message }`), `ImageSource` (an image, `Blob` or URL), `WatermarkOptions`, `ClipOrigin` and the rest are exported as types.

### `SourceInfo` and `OutputPlan`

`canClip` answers with `info: { duration, width, height, videoCodec, audioCodec, resolved, videoTracks }` (display size, rotation applied, of the largest track this browser can decode; `videoTracks` lists every track, one per HLS variant) and `plan: { audio: 'copy' | 'encode' | 'none' | 'unavailable' }`: every clip is an MP4 with H.264 and AAC, so only the audio varies (AAC copied, other audio encoded as AAC, no audio, or left out where this browser has no AAC encoder).

## `@munsonlabs/reel/vue`

`ReelPicker`, the picker as Vue single-file components on [`@munsonlabs/video-player`](/video-player/getting-started/introduction) (both peers). Inline on the page's player, it takes props `open` (`v-model:open`), `player` (the `VideoPlayer`'s handle) or `for` (the id of an `<ml-video-player>`; neither inside a `VideoPlayer`'s slot), `source` or `src` (defaults to the player's own file), `origin`, `endCard`, `watermark`; the captions burned in are the player's own, at the position chosen in the panel (`captionPosition`); emits `update:open`, `open`, `close`, `range`, `export`, `cancel`, `share`, `download`, `copy`, `error`. Its template ref has the methods and read-only state (`state`, `range`, `cropFocus`, `captionPosition`, `shareCaption`) below (`PickerApi`). Its styles are `@munsonlabs/reel/style`; the player's are `@munsonlabs/video-player/style`. Also exports `getPickerDefaults`, `setPickerDefaults` and the picker's types (`PickerApi`, `PickerState`, `PickerDefaults`, `PickerLabels`, `PickerStamp`, `CaptionPosition` (`'top' | 'middle' | 'bottom'`), `ShareCaptionInfo`, `ReelExportDetail`, `ReelErrorDetail`, `ReelCopyDetail`, `ShareResult`, `Range`).

## `@munsonlabs/reel/element`

Defines `<ml-reel-picker>` on import (add `?defer` to the specifier and call `defineReelPicker(tag?)` yourself to pick another tag): `ReelPicker` through Vue's `defineCustomElement`, without a shadow root, with reel's stylesheet put on the page once (the player's is the page's own). It finds its player through the `for` attribute (an `<ml-video-player>`'s id) or a `player` property; `src` (a URL) and `open` are attributes too. Vue and `@munsonlabs/video-player` are imported from the page. [The picker](/reel/picker/picker) documents its properties, methods, events, accessibility and theming.

| Export                                              | Description                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ReelPickerElement`                                 | The element's constructor, and its type (`HTMLElement` with the picker's properties and `PickerApi`)                   |
| `defineReelPicker(tag?)`                            | Registers it once; a second call, or a second copy of reel, is a no-op                                                 |
| `getPickerDefaults()`, `setPickerDefaults(changes)` | The page-wide labels, limits, end card, caption style, logo, stamp, display URL, share caption, watermark and filename |

`PickerLabels` (the `labels` default): `title`, `close`, `crop`, `cropHint`, `start`, `end`, `captionsUnavailable`, `thumbnailsUnavailable`, `play`, `pause`, `mute`, `unmute`, `captions`, `captionsOff`, `captionPosition`, `positionTop`, `positionMiddle`, `positionBottom`, `endCard`, `logo`, `export`, `cancel`, `preparing`, `exporting`, `cancelled`, `done`, `download`, `share`, `copyCaption`, `copied`, `copyFallback`, `captionField`, `logoUnavailable`, `captionsLeftOut`, `audioLeftOut`, `again`. Defaults are listed in [the picker's labels](/reel/picker/picker#labels).

The picker's close button is one of the player's sigil icons (the `mlv` library); see [icons](/reel/picker/picker#icons).
